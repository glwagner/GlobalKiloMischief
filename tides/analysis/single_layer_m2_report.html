The corrected convolution and spherical harmonics agree to 0.2 cm. Any SAL removes most of the 55° lag of the run without it. The leftover ~23° lag and ~23 cm error are common to all runs and do not change with resolution (0.25°: 23.0 cm), time step (5 min: 23.9 cm), or Coriolis scheme (four schemes, all 23.9–24.0 cm). They come from the single-layer physics: the stratified 10-layer 1/8° run reaches 8.7 cm and 6°.

What was fixed along the way

The asymptotic load Love numbers of Chen, Wang, Arbic & Krasny (2026) are 1.6–2.8× too strong for degrees 2–200 compared with PREM, and their MOM6 code quietly divides the kernel by 3. The NumericalEarth convolution now adds a tabulated PREM correction and the self-cell term. It reproduces spherical-harmonic SAL to 0.4% at degree 40 and passes the adjoint dot-product test to 3×10⁻¹³.
